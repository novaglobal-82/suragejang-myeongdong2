GYEONGBOKGUNG · DAKHANMARI

Slow-Simmered with Care — a Chicken Hot Pot Worth the Time

In a quiet hanok beside Gyeongbokgung Palace, Paru reinterprets Seoul-style dakhanmari: honest ingredients, honest time, and a nourishing table to restore body and mind.

01 · STORY

A New Standard in Nourishing Korean Cuisine

The quality of a true hot pot comes from ingredients with nothing to hide and time honestly spent. Paru pairs carefully selected, top-grade fresh Korean chicken with a secret bone broth slowly drawn out in a traditional medicinal pot. Simmered low and long with no artificial seasoning, the broth is clear and clean on the first sip and leaves a deep, restorative finish. As it bubbles at your table, the chicken’s own flavor and the sweetness of mushrooms and vegetables seep into the soup — a “dakhanmari of a lifetime” that restores body and mind.

02 · INGREDIENTS

Premium Chicken, Seven Mushrooms, and Refined Side Dishes

Even the best broth begins with good chicken. Paru selects only tender, springy premium Korean chicken to a strict standard, lightly pre-roasts it for extra flavor, then piles on seven kinds of mushrooms and plenty of vegetables. The longer it simmers, the more their sweetness builds into a gentle, deep umami. The same care goes into the dishes served alongside: a chilled lime and chamnamul (wild chervil) chicken salad, chicken thigh cured two days in koji salt and grilled on an iron plate, and crisp bindaetteok pancakes made from freshly ground mung beans — a table in perfect balance.

03 · FEATURE

A Modern Hanok in a Lane by Gyeongbokgung

Set in a quiet lane within walking distance of Gyeongbokgung Station, Paru is a refined hanok where you can relax over a meal before or after exploring Seochon and the palaces. From a neat lunch set to dinner gatherings and company dinners, and for international travelers looking for the depth of traditional Korean food, it has become a special place. Chicken lightly pre-roasted, a clear deep broth, and the philosophy that food is medicine — a clean meal you could eat every day. On a day you need a hearty, comforting meal, come to Paru.
